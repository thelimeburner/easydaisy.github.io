Care without the mental clutter.

EasyDaisy is local-first: the everyday essentials work on your device without an account or internet connection. Optional Cloud Sync keeps selected care records available across devices and with people you invite.

01 / ROUTINES

Know what’s next

Plan meals, walks, bathroom breaks, medication, and the rest of your dog’s day with on-device reminders.

02 / TRAINING

Make progress visible

Save skills and games, follow clear practice steps, and log the small wins that add up over time.

03 / TOGETHER

Share the routine

Optional Cloud Sync lets invited household members stay aligned on the care you choose to share.

The whole day, at a glance.

From today’s care to tomorrow’s plans and the next training session, EasyDaisy keeps the details close without getting in the way.

Private by design

Your dog’s life isn’t advertising data.

EasyDaisy does not sell personal information, use cross-app tracking, or use your data for advertising. Photos stay on your device, even when Cloud Sync is enabled.
