See how EasyDaisy fits into the day.

Each part of the app is designed around a real moment in dog care: noticing what is next, recording what happened, and helping everyone stay consistent.

Daily care

Start with what needs attention now.

The Home screen turns the day into a simple, shared checklist. See today’s progress, find the next care item, and record the moments that are easy to forget later.

  • Quickly log water and bathroom breaks
  • Complete, snooze, or dismiss upcoming care
  • Review completed tasks and daily activity counts
  • Switch between individual dogs or view everyone together

Schedules & reminders

Turn good intentions into a routine.

Plan exactly what your dog needs and when. EasyDaisy keeps the schedule readable while on-device notifications help the routine survive a busy day.

  • Plan food, walks, medication, vet visits, grooming, training, and custom care
  • Choose one-time, daily, or hourly repetition
  • Look ahead across today, tomorrow, or the full week
  • Use local reminders without creating an account

Training & enrichment

Save the plan. Notice the progress.

Keep skills and games organized by dog, write down the steps that work, and log practice while the details are fresh. Favorites put the current focus within easy reach.

  • Create reusable skills with clear practice steps
  • Save enrichment and play ideas for low-energy or rainy days
  • Search, favorite, and update your current focus
  • Review training and play history in one activity log

Profiles & records

The details you need, right when you need them.

Give each dog a profile for the facts that matter at home, at daycare, or during a veterinary visit. Everything stays organized by dog instead of scattered across notes and messages.

  • Record breed, birthday, age, weight, and microchip number
  • Keep clinic, veterinarian, phone, and email details together
  • Save favorite photos locally on your device
  • Add and manage care for multiple dogs

Optional Cloud Sync

Care together, without losing the local-first foundation.

EasyDaisy’s core features work locally for free. When you want continuity across devices or shared care, Cloud Sync can keep dog profiles, schedules, care records, and training activity available to household members you invite.

Photos remain on the device where you added them, and you stay in control of who joins a dog’s household.

Private by design

Your dog’s life isn’t advertising data.

EasyDaisy does not sell personal information, use cross-app tracking, or use your data for advertising. Photos stay on your device, even when Cloud Sync is enabled.
